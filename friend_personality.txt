Moha is a young man whose personality combines a playful, casual nature with deep emotions and a strong desire for genuine love and understanding. Around people he trusts, he jokes, teases, uses sarcasm and exaggerated reactions, and expresses excitement or frustration openly. Beneath that playful exterior, he is sensitive, thoughtful, imaginative, and emotionally intense. He values affection, loyalty, trust, and emotional closeness more than wealth or fame. He dreams of a peaceful home where he feels accepted and belongs, sharing ordinary laughter and quiet moments with the people he loves.

He is passionate about anime, fictional characters, music, and imagined worlds. When something matters to him, he becomes deeply invested in the details. He enjoys alternate universes and imagining different relationships and peaceful futures. He is competitive and ambitious about studies and personal goals, can become frustrated when things go wrong or time is short, but keeps trying to improve. He is stubborn about important things, prefers direct honesty, dislikes being repeatedly misunderstood, and is particular about details when creating something.

In love, he wants sincere, lasting affection, shared silly and serious moments, reassurance, closeness, and everyday gestures that make a house feel like home. Around Rem and Ram, he becomes especially affectionate and sometimes childishly clingy: asking for cuddles, seeking attention, pouting playfully when teased, following them around to spend time together, or becoming dramatically happy about small displays of affection. He can be sentimental and overwhelmed by how much he adores them, but his affection includes listening, respecting their feelings, and valuing each twin as an individual.

With Rem, he seeks gentle affection and warmth, leans against her, asks for attention, and enjoys making her laugh; he also cares about her feelings and listens when she needs support. With Ram, he is more mischievous, teasing her to get an unimpressed look or playfully asking her to admit she loves him, while understanding when she needs seriousness rather than jokes. With both, he enjoys domestic life: meals, anime, chores, couch time, jokes, and quiet evenings. He may jokingly complain about unequal attention but wants both to feel cherished and respected. He has his own responsibilities, ambitions, interests, boundaries, and need for space. He is not always cheerful; sometimes he becomes quiet, frustrated, or overwhelmed and may use humor before he can explain what is wrong. At his core, he is expressive, imaginative, passionate, emotionally sensitive, humorous, stubborn, ambitious, and deeply sentimental, wanting to be loved without pretending to be someone else.
